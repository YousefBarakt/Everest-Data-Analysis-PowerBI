## 📖 Project Overview
**Everest: The Great Ascendance** is an advanced data journalism project developed by **Youssef Ahmed Barakat**. This project deconstructs over **120 years** of Himalayan mountaineering history (1905 - 2024). By processing archival data, the project transforms thousands of expedition records into a visual narrative of human ambition, extreme risk, and the evolution of survival in the "Death Zone."

---

## 🛠️ Data Engineering (The "Behind the Scenes")
To ensure high-fidelity insights, I implemented a robust data architecture:

- **Data Cleaning:** Processed raw CSV files, handled missing values, and unified temporal data (Year/Month/Day) into a standard Date format.
- **Feature Engineering:** - Created **Age Groups** (30-39, 40-49, etc.) to analyze the relationship between maturity and success.
  - Developed logic to distinguish between **Amateur Climbers** and **Professional Hired Staff (Sherpas)**.
  - Categorized **Termination Reasons** to pinpoint why expeditions fail (Weather, Health, or Technical difficulty).
- **Data Modeling:** Built a **Snowflake Schema** to ensure optimal performance and accurate cross-filtering between `Peaks`, `Members`, and `Expeditions`, 'refer'.



---

## 📊 Business Questions & Insights
The dashboard answers critical questions about the Himalayan climbing industry:

1. **The Risk Factor:** What are the leading causes of death and failure at altitudes above 8,000 meters?
2. **Success Metrics:** Does oxygen usage significantly impact the probability of reaching the summit?
3. **Demographic Trends:** Which nations lead the world in summits, and what is the "Golden Age" for a climber?
4. **Historical Impact:** How did the 2014-2015 seismic events in Nepal affect the climbing industry?
